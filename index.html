Before You Book

Questions worth asking.

Why does the consultation cost money when other coaches offer free calls?

Because it's a real clinical-style assessment, not a sales pitch. We look at your current situation, markers if you have them, training, and nutrition, and decide whether the work is a fit. The fee keeps the call for people who are serious.

Do I need blood work already done before I book?

No. If you have recent panels, we use them. If you don't, we talk about what to check and when. The consultation still works either way.

I'm vegetarian. Does this actually work for me?

Yes. Several clients, including ones who started with zero gym experience, have built muscle and improved body composition on vegetarian diets. Protein sources, portion structure, and recovery are adjusted to what you actually eat.

I'm extremely busy. How much time does this actually require?

The system is built around real schedules, hospital shifts, 9-5, studies. Training is usually 3-4 sessions a week. Nutrition is structured, not complicated. The point is sustainability under constraints, not adding another full-time job.

I don't want to lose weight fast. I want to fix what's actually wrong. Is that what this is?

Yes. Fast scale drops that ignore blood markers, recovery, and muscle are the opposite of the work. We start with what's happening inside. Body composition follows as a result, not the primary target.

What if I don't have any specific health issues, just want to look and feel better?

That's still the work. Most clients come in primarily for body composition. The internal focus is what makes the external changes last and keeps energy, recovery, and long-term health from being sacrificed along the way.
